# The landing page: "Night mix"

`site/` is static (English, no build step, no framework, no library, no third-party request; fonts are local Geist / Geist Mono woff2, see [[github-pages]]) and published as is by [[github-pages]]. History: the first rebuild put a working notch on a light page; direction B made it dark and minimal; direction A (2026-11-01) kept the dark stage with far less text. On **2026-11-03** the owner chose **"Night mix"** (the mockup `docs/design/landing-v4-d-mix.html`, which stays untracked, never committed): a night sky that changes with page progress, an interactive cat in the hero, a marquee, a pinned story that grows the panel from the notch and flips through all 14 pages, and a playground of six live cards "Things other notches don't do". A fixed header with two floating pills flanks the live notch, and a cat-head link goes back to the top. The brand mark is still the terracotta cat head ([[logo]]).

Removed in this redesign: the eight feature tiles and "Also in the panel", the three screenshot sections (music, panel, clipboard) and their `site/img` files (`music.png`, `notch.png`, `clipboard.png`; grep first, only `index.html` used them, `assets/screenshots` is the README's and untouched), the Susun scrub section and `initSusun`, the "Try the cat" section with its coat and accessory pickers (and the accessory code), the hero scale and fade (`initHero`), the live notch section states music, panel, clipboard-section, cat and arrange (markup, CSS and the section IntersectionObserver), `catStep`, `dockStep`, the lyric follower, and **`fitCheck`** (`bandBlocked`, `fitFx`, `textEls`: its only users were the panel peek and the clipboard section state, so nothing referenced it any more).

Not used, on purpose: heavy cards, bento, a fake terminal window, eyebrow labels, arrows on buttons, stats, testimonials, an FAQ, em dashes (U+2014 appears nowhere under `site/`). Sentence case everywhere. No visible hint text about the cat's tricks: they are easter eggs.

## Theme and the sky

Dark is the default whatever `prefers-color-scheme` says. `:root` holds the dark set (`color-scheme: dark`), `:root[data-theme="light"]` the light set; nothing reads the system scheme. Two `button.theme-btn` (right pill, Menu dropdown); `initTheme` paints and wires both, a click flips `data-theme`, stores `vale-theme` in try/catch and asks the loop for a frame. The inline head script is byte-identical on all four pages and applies a stored light choice before the first paint.

**The sky is CSS on all four pages, with no element added to the legal pages.** `html` has an explicit fallback colour (`--sky-fallback`); `body` has **no background of its own** (an opaque body would hide the base layer; this differs from the old "body keeps an explicit background") and is a stacking context (`isolation: isolate`). Three fixed full-viewport layers: `html::before` is the night (always there, z -1), `body::before` is dusk (z -3, `opacity: calc(1 - var(--p) * 2.2)`, gone by p = .45) and `body::after` is dawn with the terracotta horizon glow (z -2, `opacity: calc(max(0, var(--p) - .62) * 2.8)`). Gradients are the mockup's. `--p` (page progress 0 to 1) is set on `<html>` by the loop only when it moved by .004; without script it stays 0, so the page shows dusk. (The mockup stacked night above dusk, which hid dusk at the top; here night is the base and dusk is on top of it.) Light: a warm pale day (`#F8F2E9` base, a soft sun glow at the top for the morning layer, a peach glow for the end layer) and the hero horizon becomes a pale sun disc. Print hides the layers.

**Stars (index only, script-created, decorative):** `canvas#sky`, `aria-hidden`, fixed, z -1 (above the three layers, behind the page). Count `round(W * H / 4200)` from a stable pool (so a resize or a phone toolbar does not reshuffle them), canvas DPR capped at 2, depth parallax on scroll (`z * .25` of the scroll), a small pointer parallax, an occasional shooting star (dark, motion welcome). Dark: warm white twinkling stars. Light: a third as many faint warm specks (`rgba(168,67,28,...)`), drawn still; meteors and the constellation are drawn in the ink `#A8431C`. Reduced motion: drawn once, again on scroll or resize.

## The walking cat and the bottom easter egg (index only)

## One loop

Exactly **one `requestAnimationFrame` call** in `site.js` (`makeLoop`, one call site, checked by count). Tasks have `read` and `write` (reads first, then writes), `on()`, `busy` and `flush`. Scroll-driven tasks (progress, header, go-to-top, marquee, story, walking cat) run only on frames where `S.dirty` says the page scrolled, resized or loaded; animation frames skip them. The loop asks for its next frame by itself only while some task is `busy`, the page is visible and motion is welcome; otherwise scroll, resize, load, pageshow, pointer moves, kicks and timers request one frame. Measured in headless Chrome: 120 frames in 2 s while visible with motion (the stars twinkle), **0 while `document.hidden`** (and 61 in the next second after it is visible again), **0 in 2 s under reduced motion** while idle; a live switch of `prefers-reduced-motion` to motion starts it (60 in 1 s) and back stops it (0). Passive listeners everywhere except the one cat `touchmove`; no wheel or touch interception, no scroll-snap, no smooth-scroll library, no per-frame `querySelectorAll`.
