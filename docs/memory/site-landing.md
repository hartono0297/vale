# The landing page: "Night mix"

`site/` is static (English, no build step, no framework, no library, no third-party request; fonts are local Geist / Geist Mono woff2, see [[github-pages]]) and published as is by [[github-pages]]. History: the first rebuild put a working notch on a light page; direction B made it dark and minimal; direction A (2026-11-01) kept the dark stage with far less text. On **2026-11-03** the owner chose **"Night mix"** (the mockup `docs/design/landing-v4-d-mix.html`, which stays untracked, never committed): a night sky that changes with page progress, an interactive cat in the hero, a marquee, a pinned story that grows the panel from the notch and flips through all 14 pages, and a playground of six live cards "Things other notches don't do". A fixed header with two floating pills flanks the live notch, and a cat-head link goes back to the top. The brand mark is still the terracotta cat head ([[logo]]).

Removed in this redesign: the eight feature tiles and "Also in the panel", the three screenshot sections (music, panel, clipboard) and their `site/img` files (`music.png`, `notch.png`, `clipboard.png`; grep first, only `index.html` used them, `assets/screenshots` is the README's and untouched), the Susun scrub section and `initSusun`, the "Try the cat" section with its coat and accessory pickers (and the accessory code), the hero scale and fade (`initHero`), the live notch section states music, panel, clipboard-section, cat and arrange (markup, CSS and the section IntersectionObserver), `catStep`, `dockStep`, the lyric follower, and **`fitCheck`** (`bandBlocked`, `fitFx`, `textEls`: its only users were the panel peek and the clipboard section state, so nothing referenced it any more).

Not used, on purpose: heavy cards, bento, a fake terminal window, eyebrow labels, arrows on buttons, stats, testimonials, an FAQ, em dashes (U+2014 appears nowhere under `site/`). Sentence case everywhere. No visible hint text about the cat's tricks: they are easter eggs.

## Theme and the sky

Dark is the default whatever `prefers-color-scheme` says. `:root` holds the dark set (`color-scheme: dark`), `:root[data-theme="light"]` the light set; nothing reads the system scheme. Two `button.theme-btn` (right pill, Menu dropdown); `initTheme` paints and wires both, a click flips `data-theme`, stores `vale-theme` in try/catch and asks the loop for a frame. The inline head script is byte-identical on all four pages and applies a stored light choice before the first paint.

**The sky is CSS on all four pages, with no element added to the legal pages.** `html` has an explicit fallback colour (`--sky-fallback`); `body` has **no background of its own** (an opaque body would hide the base layer; this differs from the old "body keeps an explicit background") and is a stacking context (`isolation: isolate`). Three fixed full-viewport layers: `html::before` is the night (always there, z -1), `body::before` is dusk (z -3, `opacity: calc(1 - var(--p) * 2.2)`, gone by p = .45) and `body::after` is dawn with the terracotta horizon glow (z -2, `opacity: calc(max(0, var(--p) - .62) * 2.8)`). Gradients are the mockup's. `--p` (page progress 0 to 1) is set on `<html>` by the loop only when it moved by .004; without script it stays 0, so the page shows dusk. (The mockup stacked night above dusk, which hid dusk at the top; here night is the base and dusk is on top of it.) Light: a warm pale day (`#F8F2E9` base, a soft sun glow at the top for the morning layer, a peach glow for the end layer) and the hero horizon becomes a pale sun disc. Print hides the layers.

**Stars (index only, script-created, decorative):** `canvas#sky`, `aria-hidden`, fixed, z -1 (above the three layers, behind the page). Count `round(W * H / 4200)` from a stable pool (so a resize or a phone toolbar does not reshuffle them), canvas DPR capped at 2, depth parallax on scroll (`z * .25` of the scroll), a small pointer parallax, an occasional shooting star (dark, motion welcome). Dark: warm white twinkling stars. Light: a third as many faint warm specks (`rgba(168,67,28,...)`), drawn still; meteors and the constellation are drawn in the ink `#A8431C`. Reduced motion: drawn once, again on scroll or resize.

## The hero cat

Inline SVG in the logo's 256 grid (the paths of `site/img/logo.svg`) with separate ears, eyes, happy `^ ^` eyes, squint `> <` eyes, a calico patch group (clipped to the head) and a rim. Without script it is a static `role=img` with an aria-label ("Vale's cat, a cartoon cat head"); script makes the wrapper `role=button`, `tabindex=0`, `aria-label` "Vale's cat. Press to poke it". Colours come from six custom properties on `<html>` (`--coat --earl --earr --eye --rim --pa`), which the hero cat, the live notch's idle and happy cats (`<symbol id="cat-idle">`, `cat-happy`, `cat-sleep` in the sprite), the picture cats and the walking cat all read, so one chip changes all of them.

**DECIDED coats for the site** (cat-colours-and-surfaces has the app's): Terracotta head #E2683C, eyes #17120F; Calico head #F4EDE4, orange #E2683C patch and left ear, dark #2B2420 patch and right ear; White #F6F1EA; **Black #1E1B22, amber eyes #F2B33D, rim #75675B** (the request's choice for the site; the app's rim is #8E7CB8, so the two differ on purpose). The logo files and the header brand stay terracotta.

Behaviour (as in the mockup): eyes follow the pointer (eased, tau 110 ms), the head tilts up to 6 degrees, blink every 2.5 to 6 s, the right ear twitches every 4 to 9 s, ears perk on hover (CSS). About 0.7 s after load a bubble greets by local time with the app's periods ([[greeting-periods]]: 00:00-03:59 "Up late?", 04:00-10:59 "Good morning!", 11:00-17:59 "Good afternoon!", 18:00-23:59 "Good evening!"). Click, Enter or Space: a squash-and-stretch jump (Web Animations), a bubble cycling "mrrp!", "meow", "hi!", "nya~", sparks. **Rubbing** with a mouse or pen and no button down (path length `pet = pet * exp(-dt / 545 ms) + distance`, on at 260, off below 120, event-driven, never touch): happy eyes, a fine vibration, hearts. **Drag:** mouse press and move more than 6 px; touch long press 350 ms then move. A non-passive `touchmove` listener on the cat element only calls `preventDefault` while a drag is armed or running, so a quick swipe over the cat still scrolls (checked: swipe scrolled 185 px, the cat did not move); the long-press `contextmenu` is suppressed. Dragged: squint eyes, it follows the pointer, tilts with the velocity (clamped to 25 degrees), is raised above the header (z 95, under the notch's 100; `.hero-inner` is its own stacking context, so the script also puts `.is-carrying` (z 95) on it while the cat is held); released it springs home (60 Hz steps). **Dropped on the notch** (box test: within 110 px of its centre and above its bottom + 70): the notch glows (`.is-glow`) while the cat is over it, the cat shrinks into it, the notch shows the **greet state** for about 2 s, the cat pops back and says "again?". `notchApi.hold(2800)` stops the panel from hover-opening over the greeting (the pointer is still on the notch after the drop; found in testing). **The wish:** five presses within 2 s: the cat looks up, 14 meteors, then 15 stars fly into the cat's head constellation (`CON_PTS`, 13 `CON_LINES`, centred behind the cat at 1.8 times its size), lines drawing one after another, a hold, a fade; 7.4 s in all; bubbles "make a wish" and "granted". The look-up and the fade are timed in the loop, not CSS.

**Reduced motion:** no jump, sparks, hearts, vibration, ear twitch, meteors or constellation; bubbles and eye changes still work; the wish shows only "make a wish"; a drag snaps home; the drop shows no shrink. Checked: 0 particles, 0 running animations on the cat, one bubble, 785 static star pixels, and no "granted".

## Go to top

**A cartoon cat tail, no disc (2026-11-03, "site tail to top").** `<span id="top" tabindex="-1">` is the first element in `body`; `a.to-top[href="#top"][aria-label="Back to top"]` comes after the footer, identical on all four pages (`check-site.py` compares the whole markup). It holds one inline svg, `viewBox="-4 0 56 100"`, `width=56 height=100`, `aria-hidden`, no asset and no request: `g.tail` with six paths drawn in this order: two `tail-halo` (stroke 15), two `tail-edge` (stroke 12), then `tail-body` (stroke 9, `var(--coat)`) and `tail-tip` (stroke 9, `var(--tip)`). The body is `M52 92 C 30 90, 22 70, 30 52 C 38 34, 26 18, 14 14` (an S, flush at x = 52, which is the right edge of the box and of the window, because the svg clips what lies past it) and the tip a separate curl `M14 14 C 6 12, 4 22, 12 24`, all round caps and joins, fill none. The viewBox pads 4 units on the left so the swish stays inside the box. The link box is 56 x 100, `position: fixed; right: 0`, bottom 16 px below 40em and 24 px from 40em up, `display: block`, z 80 (under the header 90 and the notch 100). Focus: the site's `3px solid var(--focus)` with `outline-offset: -3px`, so all four sides of the ring show on the flush edge (looked at in a crop).

**Colours.** The body follows the coat; the tip is the new `--tip` property, in `:root` (default #F4EDE4) and in every `COATS` entry that `setCoat` writes: terracotta #F4EDE4 cream, calico #E2683C (one of its patch colours), white #E3D8CA (a darker cream), black #4B4650 (a dark grey). **Outline, two rings, because one ring was not enough.** First version: one underlay ring (`--tail-edge`: #75675B dark, #17120F light, the notch-edge idea). Measured against every rendered pixel behind it (the sweep method in "Tokens", text, stars, header, notch and walking cat hidden), it failed: dark 1.91 over a lifted card and picture surface at 390 (a 626 px cluster), light 1.00 over the dark story screen and the playground pictures, which are dark in both themes. So there are two rings: `--tail-edge` is the ring that contrasts with the theme's page (dark #A89A8B, 6.6 on #17120F; light #17120F) and `--tail-halo` is the opposite tone outside it (dark #17120F, light #F6F6F4). Over a dark surface the light ring is the visible edge, over a light one the dark ring is, and the halo's own colour merges with a page of that tone, so nothing looks like a pill. A pixel passes if the better of the two rings reaches 3:1 against the pixel behind it. **Result (index with the four coats chosen through the chips, privacy, terms; every 0.5 viewport of scroll where the tail is shown, three swish poses, -2.5, 0 and +2.5 degrees):** lowest 3.71 dark and 7.56 light at 390 x 844, 4.68 dark and 13.10 light at 1280 x 800 (index; the coat does not change it, the rings are theme tokens). Privacy 4.68 / 13.10 (390) and 4.99 / 14.09 (1280), terms 4.68 / 13.10 and 5.84 / 14.97. Without the outline the coat body alone was 1.00 for calico and white (light) and black (dark), 2.36 for terracotta (light) and 3.04 (dark, 390), so the outline is needed for all four. The 404 page is shorter than a viewport, so the tail never shows there (checked: 0 shown steps).

**Show and hide.** With script it is hidden until the hero's bottom is above the viewport (one viewport height on pages without a hero) and hidden again near the top (same `.is-shown`, same loop task in `initToTop`). Hidden: `transform: translateX(100%)`, `visibility: hidden`, `pointer-events: none`, so it is neither a tab stop nor a hit target. Shown: `translateX(0)` in .35 s with `cubic-bezier(.34, 1.56, .64, 1)` (it overshoots by roughly 5 px to the left, so for a moment a gap of that size opens at the right edge; at rest the box right edge equals `clientWidth`). Slide out: .35 s ease-in with `visibility 0s linear .35s`, so the slide can be seen (the show rule sets that delay to 0). `.js.reveal-off .to-top` still forces it visible (transform none, visibility visible, pointer-events auto). Print hides it, as before. Without script it is always visible, still, with no animation (checked at 390 and 1280 on index, privacy and 404: visible, no transform, 0 animations, box right edge = `clientWidth`, no overflow, the link goes to #top).

**The covers test uses the SHOWN box.** The "hide while over" list is unchanged (`.btn, .coat-chip, .play-card button, .more summary, .cmd-scroll, .site-footer a`, built once), but the box it is compared with is worked out from `offsetLeft`, `offsetTop`, `offsetWidth` and `offsetHeight` of the fixed link in the loop task's `read`, never from `getBoundingClientRect()`. Checked at 390 on index (349 scroll positions, 20 px apart): `is-shown` matched an independent recomputation everywhere (0 mismatches), 38 positions were covered (Download 7, Copy 18, a details summary 13) and the tail was hidden there; scrolled on, it slides back. **Motion** (all under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` and `.js`; transform only, no rAF, no loop task `busy`). The link carries the slide and the yank; the inner `g.tail` carries the swish, `transform-origin: 52px 92px` (the base on the right edge, in user units), so they never fight and the flush clip does not rotate. **Geometry checks.** 5 viewports (360 x 780, 390 x 844, 430 x 932, 768 x 1024, 1280 x 800) x dark and light x the four pages, scrolled in steps of 0.6 of the viewport: `scrollWidth <= clientWidth` everywhere (a fixed element does not add scroll overflow, and the slide and the yank are inward or vertical), the box is 56 x 100, its right edge equals `clientWidth` once shown, it is hidden at the top and after scrolling back, shown only after the hero (index) or one viewport (others), and the link, svg and paths have a computed border radius 0, no background, no border and no shadow. ## One loop

Exactly **one `requestAnimationFrame` call** in `site.js` (`makeLoop`, one call site, checked by count). Tasks have `read` and `write` (reads first, then writes), `on()`, `busy` and `flush`. Scroll-driven tasks (progress, header, go-to-top, marquee, story, walking cat) run only on frames where `S.dirty` says the page scrolled, resized or loaded; animation frames skip them. The loop asks for its next frame by itself only while some task is `busy`, the page is visible and motion is welcome; otherwise scroll, resize, load, pageshow, pointer moves, kicks and timers request one frame. Measured in headless Chrome: 120 frames in 2 s while visible with motion (the stars twinkle), **0 while `document.hidden`** (and 61 in the next second after it is visible again), **0 in 2 s under reduced motion** while idle; a live switch of `prefers-reduced-motion` to motion starts it (60 in 1 s) and back stops it (0). Passive listeners everywhere except the one cat `touchmove`; no wheel or touch interception, no scroll-snap, no smooth-scroll library, no per-frame `querySelectorAll`. Parts: theme, menu, copy, coats, mini clocks, sky and stars, notch, hero cat, marquee, header, go-to-top, story, the six cards, walking cat, reveal; each in `safe()`; `ready` is set last. **Reveal** (h2, h3, figure and `article.play-card`; once, by IntersectionObserver; cards stagger by 60 ms in a row of three).
